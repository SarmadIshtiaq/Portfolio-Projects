## 2. Business Purpose

**Business request**

Customer Success wants to pilot an AI-enabled SaaS tool that summarises customer support calls and tickets.

**Intended business activity**

Generate summaries from customer-support interactions to support the Customer Success workflow.

**Business problem**

The assignment brief identifies the requested summarisation activity but does not quantify the existing manual workload, current processing time, or expected productivity improvement.

These details require confirmation from the business owner.

**Expected business outcomes to validate**

- Whether the service reduces manual summarisation effort.
- Whether support documentation becomes more consistent.
- Whether employees can access useful summaries more efficiently.
- Whether the generated summaries preserve important customer information.
- Whether the proposed service creates measurable operational benefits.

These are evaluation objectives, not confirmed benefits.

## 12. Initial AI Risk Classification

The following risk areas have been identified for further assessment.

| AI Risk Area | Relevance to Use Case | Assessment Status |
|---|---|---|
| Confabulation | Generated summaries may contain unsupported statements. | Assessment required |
| Data Privacy | Customer information may be processed by an external service. | Assessment required |
| Human-AI Configuration | Employees must retain meaningful review and accountability. | Assessment required |
| Information Integrity | Summaries must preserve the meaning of source interactions. | Assessment required |
| Information Security | Customer information requires appropriate protection. | Assessment required |
| Third-Party Integration | AI providers and subprocessors may introduce dependencies. | Assessment required |

The risk areas are drawn from the assignment brief and will be assessed using [NIST AI RMF 1.0](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) and [NIST AI 600-1 — Generative AI Profile](https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-generative-artificial-intelligence).

They are not confirmed control failures or final risk ratings.
