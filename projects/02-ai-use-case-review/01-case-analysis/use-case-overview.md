## 1. Purpose

This document describes the proposed use of an AI-enabled Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) tool within Avenloft Systems Group's Customer Success function.

The business has requested approval to pilot a tool that summarises customer support calls and tickets.

The purpose of this review is to understand the proposed use case before making a governance decision.

The review will document the business purpose, intended users, AI functionality, input information, expected outputs, third-party dependencies, human oversight, and approved-use boundaries.

**Primary assignment source:** Avenloft Systems Group — GRC-AI-002 training case brief.

## 8. Human Oversight

Human oversight must be assessed as part of the proposed use case.

The review must establish:

- Who reviews generated summaries.
- Whether review is required before a summary is saved.
- Whether employees can compare a summary with its source.
- Whether inaccurate or unsupported statements can be corrected.
- Whether corrections are recorded.
- Who remains accountable for the final customer-support record.

An AI-generated summary must not automatically be treated as an accurate record of a customer interaction.

The case brief illustrates a possible confabulation scenario: an AI-generated summary could incorrectly state that a customer restarted a system even though the original transcript does not contain that information.

This example demonstrates why source verification and meaningful human review matter.
